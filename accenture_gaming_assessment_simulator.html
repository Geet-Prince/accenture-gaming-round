Accenture Gamified Cognitive Assessment Arena

Each game consists of 15 progressively tougher levels inside a 15-minute overall game timer, with a strict 1-minute (60s) countdown per question. Every time you start or re-attempt a round, our procedural engine generates 100% brand-new questions so you never memorize static answers.

How Our Infinite Re-Attempt Randomizer & Dual-Timer Work

15:00 Round + 01:00 Question You have 15 minutes total for all 15 levels of a game, and a strict 60-second timer for each individual level. If 60s expires in Exam Mode, it auto-advances to the next level!
Never Repeats on Re-Attempt Whether you re-attempt Rat / Memory Maze, Bubble Math, or Key & Door Maze, every single attempt generates a new cryptographic seed (`SEED #XXXX`), creating a brand-new grid, path, or expression set.
BFS Solvability Verified Every generated maze, pipe network, and key/door puzzle is solved behind the scenes by a Breadth-First Search (BFS) verifier before rendering to guarantee zero deadlocks.
Competition Scoring Earn base points scaled by level difficulty (L1–L15) plus a Per-Second Speed Bonus based on how much of your 60-second question timer remains.
